Architecture
------------
Only75 original primary front locks receive new lower front surfaces. Their
existing center curves, rear section points, contour endpoints, and upper
geometry remain. Sections use the old8-sided polygon above the transition;
lower rings have20 illuminated-front edges plus the original4 rear edges.
The front surfaces replace the original front; no extra opaque tube or plane
is overlaid. Cross sections remain closed. Tapered open end topology/alpha is
inherited; this is not a claim that the full original groom is watertight.

Twenty-five shared family fields make unequal2–4 lobes. Each valley follows
the accepted family center paths in authored X/Y and reaches every covering
front section. Inward Z capacity is bounded by actual original triangle rays,
fixed neighboring hair, and a24% vertical half-depth floor. All MASS X/Y
positions and original rear/contour ring points are unchanged. Onsets and
releases vary smoothly; the same original maps and materials are reused.

The2700 existing forward fine curves now sample the actual rebuilt raw front
triangles through their shared evaluator, distributed by section arc length.
Existing counts, random draws, widths, colors, UVs, and trailing phase choices
are retained. A guarded transition keeps upper fine positions AND normals
byte-identical. Rear fine curves and all other fine/crown/fringe/wisp/far/side
components remain unchanged. Fine positions are intentionally redistributed
and resampled; maximum source XYZ changes are.0260/.0419/.0335 and should be
judged in the profiles for unwanted bends or separated highlights.
