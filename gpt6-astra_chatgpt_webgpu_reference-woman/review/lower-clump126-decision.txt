Both variants use the updated130 baseline, Neutral1.4, hidden closed-mouth

Front: the lower lengths acquire somewhat stronger narrow channels, but
their broad paired mass and repeated, similarly curved pointed locks remain
dominant. The reference has irregular groups that merge, divide, and overlap
at several scales.126 adds relief inside the old lock arrangement without
achieving that change in structure. There is no clear likeness improvement
at the normal portrait size.

Profiles: the hanging front sections still stand forward of the shirt. The
new channels add thin corrugated ribs and sharper dark separations through
the middle/lower lengths; these look more constructed than the original
rounder strips. Their continuity is technically coherent, but the visible
result still resembles repeated extruded ribbons rather than soft clumps.

Relit front: some channel edges become bright, separated cuts, particularly
in the lower right-hand length and around the lower turn on the left. This
is greater groove visibility, not convincing individual fiber variation.
The underlying broad-sheet appearance survives the change in lighting.

Protected views: no gross face/crown/fringe/back regression is apparent.
The existing crown cap, rear curtain, lower silhouette repetition, and
profile hair/cloth crescent remain substantive realism limits.

Cost/decision: native construction1904.1 to1974.3ms is about70.2ms additional;
62,224 extra triangles and2.04MiB are modest costs, but the visual benefit
does not support acceptance. Successful geometry checks do not change this
art verdict. Keep the study frozen; no more strength tuning, optics changes,
export work, or source auditing is warranted for126 before another design.

Implication for a future structural study: the actual family silhouette and
overlap order need an irregular hierarchy. More subdivisions or deeper
valleys inside the same75 lock shells will keep the current repeated shape
and may sharpen corrugation. Any successor should first demonstrate a small
coherent family replacement with naturally unequal merge/split and tip
behavior, using unchanged materials, before expanding across the groom.
