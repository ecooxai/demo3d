Field and presets
  F(X,Y,Z)=(X,Y,Z+D(Y,Z)) in complete-model local coordinates.
  D=w(Y)*(A*exp(-((Z-.75)/.54)^2)-B*exp(-((Z+.55)/.54)^2)).
  0: exact no-op, no traversal, matrix creation or geometry allocations.
  .5 mild: A=.18, B=.05. This is a named mild preset, not half amplitude.
  1 full: A=.24, B=.08.
  Intermediate strengths interpolate continuously through those presets.
  C2 quintic bottom fade: zero at/below−1.28, full by−.10.
  C2 quintic upper fade: full through+.10, zero at/above1.60.
  The actual maximum positive displacement is .179849/.239758 because the
  small opposite Gaussian tail is retained. No uniform depth scaling.

  The depth displacement peaks at the torso and falls toward outer hair.
  X/Y remain fixed up to unavoidable Float32 storage roundoff.
  Every mesh uses this same field, including neck, collars and buttons.
  Normals use the analytic inverse transpose after conjugating the field
  through full mesh/model matrices. Optional tangents transform forwards.
  Same-strength reapplication returns the original metrics; a changed strength
  requires a fresh model. All changed geometry is staged before assignment.
  UV0, UV1, colors, indices, materials and texture channels remain exact.
  No new triangles, maps, draw calls, texture assets or baseline buffers remain.
