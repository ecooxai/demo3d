## What the source supports, and what remains uncertain

The left field explicitly blends its cubic root routes into the retained principal-by-height field using the same `1-smooth(.45,.70,Y)` interval for every lane. The native synchronized turn is consistent with this concentrated, shared-height directional correction. The recorded maximum positional correction is about **.2788**. This is a source-based explanation, not a separately rendered causal isolation.

The 16/8 regularly spaced ribbons have varying end heights, but each uses the same taper-to-one-tip construction. Their half-width and edge alpha are reduced independently along that arrangement. The source coverage gate already retained oblique misses; the rendered long pale channels show why sampled support and valid triangle orientation were insufficient to establish convincing interior coverage. The geometry remains too uniformly organized despite unequal numerical endpoints.

The broad root panels are actual visible coarse surfaces. They share a depth support field that can lift the initial route by **.3171**, while the added transverse relief reaches only **.00540**. Those facts are consistent with broad shallow sheets and strong root-to-support transitions. They do not establish that every tonal panel is caused solely by geometry. The new mesh intentionally uses the ordinary MASS material with its own arclength UV/tangent mapping; the original 100 CROWN response is not its final shading equivalent. No normal-map, material, or alpha isolation was rendered. Such effects can amplify the appearance, but the visible kink, outline and gap failures justify rejection without an optical rescue.
