Scope and construction
----------------------
The original146 front/curl/inner guides are rebuilt before their MASS sections
and dependent fine paths. The root-approved four clear side guides154/155/166/167
follow the same field; the other20 side guides, all106 rear guides, and14 far
temple guides are unchanged. All authored guide center X/Y are exactly retained.
The source geometry above authoredY-1.64 is exactly retained; the guide field
starts below-1.74 and the independent fine-curve interpolation has an upper guard.

Each side uses one C2 bicubic B-spline depth-translation field fitted against the
accepted blouse and accepted86 shoulderContact .35. Coefficient caps are .22/.32.
The original section axes are transported by the field Jacobian before sweeping.
This retains MASS section X/Y and depth offsets, with depth derivative and
Jacobian determinant1. Normals are rebuilt from the swept geometry. It does not
apply a final-mesh warp, compress lock depth, add surfaces, or change the RNG.
LongLock is deferred in the original order; the zero case is byte-identical to
the original source geometry, colors, UVs, normals and indices.

The fixed side locks are temporary triangle obstacles in the fit. Whole source
depth intervals are compared, so already interleaved pairs are not falsely
treated as separate layers. Source gaps below.005 are not claimed as separated.
The fitted field has broad support and does not switch to individual hit normals.
There is no explicit source-tip-depth constraint; inherited tight cloth contact
still limits permissible movement near several ends.
