The reference has overlapping root and strand groups that continue into soft, unequally separated forehead hair.119 still shows a broad opaque cap ending above thinner, visibly distinct brush-like families. The intervention changed the part above this junction; it did not reconstruct the junction itself.

## What the successful source audit did establish

The native runs completed with WebGPU and no reported page errors. Source checks found no new flipped crown-sheet triangles, preserved the existing geometry/strand budget, and verified that sampled slit rays hit the inset foundation. Protected positions below original localY1.05 were unchanged.

Those checks established structural safety and isolation. They did **not** establish a natural silhouette, correct visual thickness of the banks, or a source-faithful crown-to-fringe transition. In particular, the dark notch is not contradicted by the foundation-support test: the inset floor is precisely what can become visible as an artificial dark cut.

## Consequence for the next pass

Do not increase119’s relief, add more small ridges, enlarge its slit, or adjust its color to disguise the result. Retain119 as a rejected, frozen experiment. The next structural study should replace the affected front crown and fringe construction together, so the dense root region and sparse ends share actual continuous paths and the old cap edge ceases to be a visible contour.

Candidate118’s accepted half-strength lower-hair placement is independent. Its registration and Jacobian deformation should remain unchanged in that next study.
