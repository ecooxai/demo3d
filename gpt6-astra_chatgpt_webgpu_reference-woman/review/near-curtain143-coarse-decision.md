## Proposed minimum scope adjustment

Ask the parent to authorize only the lower portions of side154 and side155, and their 56 dependent fine strands, as rear companions in the same coupled continuum. Keep their accepted upper routes exact and retain all remaining side/back, crown, fringe and far-curtain protections. This would change the scope from 67 to 69 guides and from 2,191 to 2,247 base fine strands.

The design must plan their layer depth before meshing and carry the same section volume and dependent-fiber evaluation; it must not hide the conflict with an overlap tolerance, a new backing sheet or a large front lift. A further coarse native-ready allocation is not justified until the coupled transition clears the actual side-volume conflict without reintroducing an outward shoulder bridge. The proposed scope adjustment is not yet implemented or approved in this record.
