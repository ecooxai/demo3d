## Visible result

The isolated garment and collar oblique views show the useful effect more clearly: each free leaf reads as a broader bowed cloth surface, with a small return at the point rather than a completely straight pointed panel. The near leaf has a softer transition into its free edge. This is a restrained improvement in collar shape, not a major correction to overall likeness or cotton drape.

Neck seating, button placement, blouse folds and hair silhouettes appear stable.

## Remaining limits

The fixed collar roots still sit high and read somewhat squared at the neck. Both leaves retain broad triangular areas, and the blouse still lacks the reference's full irregular cotton fold hierarchy. These limitations remain visible and prevent treating the modest collar change as a scene score increase.
