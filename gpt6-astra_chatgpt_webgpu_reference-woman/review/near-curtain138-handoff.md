- Protected positions and normals have **zero differences**, including the required upper region. Finite values pass. Maximum unit-normal length error is `4.97e-08`; the same3 pre-existing zero normals remain.
- Source MASS triangle samples:72,851 body hits, minimum normal gap `0.082662`, median `0.191646`;8,455 collar/facing hits, minimum depth gap `0.038332`. No sampled body/collar penetration was found.

## Remaining upper shoulder overlap

Actual Float32 MASS samples were checked against individual closed side-lock front/rear intervals, not just the maximum front envelope. Across75,413 samples and6,712 front-envelope hits:

- Fully joined lower section minimum front separation: `0.022706`.
- `1631` sampled point/side pairs lie within a fixed side interval.
- `803` are newly inside relative to the corresponding accepted118.5 old section.
- `1030` worsen their interval depth by more than0.003; these counts overlap the previous category.
- Maximum measured interval depth / worsening: `0.048632`.

The largest new contacts occur in the upper transition around model Y1.35–1.55. Most involve near inner guides179–199 and fixed side guides148–155. Some source overlaps were inherited, but the new ones cannot be labeled an exact clearance pass. They may be hidden inside the combined groom; the near profile/curtain closeup must determine whether they create a visible shoulder seam, crossing or abrupt flow. The full raw observations are in `review/near-curtain138-obstacles.json`.

No protected side guide was silently moved, and no corrective post-mesh compression was added. The native experiment is diagnostic; integration should not ignore this finding even if the front view improves.

## Art acceptance

Look first for a useful ordinary-size change in unequal major clumps, two stronger long sweeps, longitudinal separations and naturally different ends. Check the continuous shoulder-to-chest path in both profiles, especially the flagged upper transition. Reject a collection of narrow uniform tubes, synchronized hooks, visible crossings, large shoulder gaps or a worse lower silhouette. The+16.96MiB cost needs a clear visible benefit. No score increase is justified by these source checks alone.
