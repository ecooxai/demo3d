Why the lower lengths still read as a constructed wig
----------------------------------------------------
The reference has a few broad flowing masses, then unequal smaller groups within
them, then fine individual hairs. Dark channels travel with those groups, change
width, and fade or combine at different heights. They are not evenly spaced
parallel grooves across the whole bundle.

Current source front guides have similar widths (.059-.071 half-width), eight
vertices around each elliptical section, and common large paths in three-lane
families. The wide inner guides maintain a second smooth surface beneath them.
Forty-three front fine strands per lock mostly sit on the same ellipse. The result
has a large smooth face with small streaks laid along it.118 improves placement,
but it does not change that section structure.

111 puts83 ridges around a single texture circumference. Much of this is finer
than a useful visible clump at the ordinary portrait size; its native front still
reads as the same broad locks.121 amplifies the optical response and exposes
repeated ladder-like highlights.107 adds photographic wave structure in a
repeating UV field, which creates diagonal fingerprints on the rear sheets.
Those experiments do not supply the missing intermediate clump geometry.

Recommended single structural experiment
---------------------------------------
Replace the exposed elliptical surface of the75 primary front locks with closed,
asymmetric multi-lobe sections, and rebuild their existing fine paths on exactly
that section. Keep the original center guides and accepted118.5 placement.
Start below authoredY-1.64; crown, ear-height lengths, part, fringe, side and back
guides remain protected. The56 inner front guides remain the baseline support in
the first experiment, rather than gaining another overlaid surface.

Each existing front lock remains one continuous mesh component. Its illuminated
cross-section acquires2-4 unequal lobes separated by1-3 genuine concave channels.
Use one dominant lobe and smaller neighboring ones, with irregular width ratios;
vary the count and relative widths across the existing three-lane families. The
channels must not appear as the same repeated M-shaped extrusion on every lock.

Use a shared sectionPoint(g,t,lane,side) evaluator for MASS and all its dependent
fine paths. Retain the old silhouette extrema and rear half of the section.
Recess the front between lobes, rather than adding raised opaque strips above
the original smooth parent. The old front face is replaced, so it cannot fill
its own grooves. Cap the recess as a fraction of actual front-to-back thickness;
at all samples the front remains above the retained rear surface. Target a
visible3-8 pixel group width at the ordinary native portrait, with channel
recesses around.012-.024 model units where the parent thickness permits them.
Near tapered ends, scale the recess with section thickness and fade it gently.

Each channel follows a smooth, slowly changing lane inside its parent guide.
Its beginning, strongest region and ending are staggered across families. Use
one or two broad cubic transitions; do not add longitudinal sinusoidal cycles,
per-row phases, or a repeated shared start/end band. The parent guide supplies
the main wave. Channels can converge modestly, but should not cross or create
an additional outward tip hook. The original source-facing outer contour stays
the baseline silhouette.

Redistribute the existing fine paths by arc length over the new exposed section,
including its sloped walls. Do not leave them suspended on the old ellipse.
Keep their current count, material, width distribution and RNG stream. Use an
independent deterministic seed for new section parameters. Normal seams should
be welded, and the surface should have continuous geometric normals along its
length. Keep accepted material settings and original maps for the first render.

Sampling and topology
---------------------
Eight circumference samples cannot describe this section smoothly. Use24 samples
for the altered lower region, preferably subdividing existing source ring edges
at the start of the transition so that the upper geometry is identical. Keep
the existing60 longitudinal intervals unless a measured interpolation defect
requires more. A conservative all-ring24-sample estimate for75 guides adds
144,000 triangles and about4.7MiB of vertex/index buffers; a lower-only adaptive
layout should cost less. There are no new draw calls or textures, and the fine
strand count stays fixed. Active MASS topology will change deliberately; source
guide ranges must replace any audit assumption that every guide has549 vertices.

Compatibility with118 and contact
--------------------------------
Fit the accepted118.5 field against its original parent envelopes first. Then
construct the modified sections inside those envelopes using its transported
axes. Keeping the rear support and silhouette avoids spending the clearance
gained by118. It also avoids moving the embedded side locks that constrained the
placement study. Check actual new triangles against cloth and protected locks;
containment in a smooth conceptual ellipse is not a substitute for checking the
original finite mesh. Preserve all protected components, colors/maps and upper
surface geometry, and regenerate only the modified front fine paths.

Before native rendering, check a sparse set of actual front/profile rays through
the changed lower locks. A new valley that is immediately filled by an unchanged
neighbor or inner sheet will not produce the intended visible channel. Report
that exposed-surface coverage; do not respond by increasing shine or painting
stronger stripes. If most intended channels are buried, the needed next scope
is a coherent family envelope across the overlapping locks, not more detail on
hidden geometry. That is the principal feasibility risk of this bounded test.

Native acceptance criteria
--------------------------
Front: the lower lengths read as irregular nested groups at ordinary preview
size. Channels follow the established waves and have unequal widths and lengths.
The apparent improvement must persist with the accepted diffuse lighting; it
must not depend on isolated white highlights. Source silhouette and cotton gaps
should stay close to the118.5 baseline.

Profiles: the lengths retain their mild accepted drape, thickness and continuous
flow. Reject a ribbed hose, flat plates, serrated contour, repeated transverse
glints, a newly detached loop, or renewed unsupported gaps.

Detail/back: face, crown, fringe and rear remain visually unchanged. A relit front
can distinguish real recessed structure from a fortuitous highlight, but the
ordinary front and both profiles remain the primary art decision.

Reject the experiment early if it is imperceptible at normal size, merely adds
regular parallel ribs, or exposes flat inner sheets in the valleys. A technical
pass alone does not improve the76 score. No claim is made that this one change
can reach95; it addresses one substantial unresolved structural defect.
