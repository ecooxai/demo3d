Yuukapa

A fully procedural 3D reconstruction of the reference illustration — indigo mane, violet anime eyes, cat-print pyjamas, scalloped bloomer hem, halo, bow and pillow. Every vertex and every texture is generated in code; nothing is imported.

WebGPU first

WebGPURenderer with an explicit WebGL 2 fallback — the app asks before it ever drops to another backend.

Toon NPR

Four-step cel ramp, inverted-hull ink outlines and fresnel rim shells over a sculpted, painted face.

Procedural everything

Swept ribbons, lofts, super-ellipsoids and Canvas2D textures — built by src/js/lib.

Any screen

900×1200 by default, selectable up to 4K, with orbit, camera presets and PNG/webm capture.
